What if Earth had rings like Saturn? Look up at night, and a glowing arch would stretch across the whole sky. You would see it in the daytime too, pale and enormous. The rings would be made of ice and rock, circling above the equator. Their shadow would fall across half the planet, so some places would probably get colder, darker winters. Satellites would have a real problem. Any orbit that crossed the rings would get smashed by countless fragments. So rockets would launch from far north or far south instead. And here's the strange part. Rings don't last forever. Saturn's rings are slowly raining down onto the planet. Scientists think they could be mostly gone in a few hundred million years.
