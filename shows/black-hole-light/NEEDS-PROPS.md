# Needs props: How does a black hole bend light?

The writer stopped: no prop in the 3D catalogue can show this topic's central mechanism changing, and icons or bar charts are not an honest stand-in.

## The mechanism prop that is needed

A curved-light prop: a glowing ray (or beaded light_wave) that travels from a distant star past a dark massive sphere (a black hole or star with a faint gravity-well glow) toward the viewer, and whose path visibly bends toward the mass. One value, 'bend' (0 to 1), sets how far the path curves: at 0 the ray is perfectly straight, at 1 it curves tightly around the sphere and nearly wraps it. A second value, 'mass' (small to huge), sets how strongly the ray is pulled, so the viewer sees the same ray straighten when the mass shrinks and curl when it grows. The existing beam and light_wave only run in a straight line between two points and the black_hole only varies its disc, so none of them can show a bending path.
