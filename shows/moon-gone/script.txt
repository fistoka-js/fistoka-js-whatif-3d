What if the Moon vanished tonight? The first thing you would notice is the sky. Without moonlight, nights would be much darker, and the stars would shine brighter. Then the oceans would change. The Moon pulls on our seas and makes the tides. Without it, only the Sun's weaker pull would remain, and tides would shrink to less than half their size. Coasts and the creatures living there would struggle. The biggest effect would come slowly. The Moon helps keep Earth's tilt steady, and that tilt gives us regular seasons. Without it, the tilt could probably wobble over millions of years, and the climate could swing wildly. Earth would survive, but life would have to adapt.
