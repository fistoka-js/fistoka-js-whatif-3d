# Storyboard: How does a battery actually light a bulb?

- Hook: How does a battery light a bulb?
- Answer the video gives: The battery pushes electrons around a closed loop of wire, out along one wire, through the thin filament in the bulb where they crash into atoms and heat it white-hot, and back along the other wire.
- Hero prop: wire
- Reviewer: PASS

We open on a big battery and a small bulb, then pull back to a full loop: a battery on the left, a bulb on the right, one wire carrying glowing dots from the battery to the bulb and a second wire carrying them back. Next we move into the bulb: electrons INSIDE the glass squeeze into the filament, bump the metal atoms, and a thermometer shows the heat reaching two thousand degrees as the filament turns bright white. A crowd squeezing through a gap is the analogy. Last we return to the loop, cut it so the dots stop and the bulb goes dark, close it so it relights, and end on the broken wire with the dots piled up at the gap.
