# Storyboard: What If Earth Suddenly Stopped Spinning?

- Hook: What if Earth stopped spinning?
- Answer the video gives: The ground would stop but the air and oceans would not, so huge winds would scour the land, the seas would drain toward the poles, and each day would last a year.
- Hero prop: planet
- Reviewer: PASS (after 2 repair(s))

A big Earth is shown first with a speed gauge reading about one thousand miles an hour, then the gauge drops to zero as the spin stops. On the ground a boulder and a tree are hit by a wall of fast wind and dust. Back in space, the ocean swell slides to the pole and leaves dry land around the middle, and the sun lights only one side. A toy bus braking while its crates slide forward explains why. The video ends on a calm Earth and the question of where you would stand.
