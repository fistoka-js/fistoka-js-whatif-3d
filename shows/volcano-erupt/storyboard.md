# Storyboard: Why do volcanoes really erupt?

- Hook: Why do volcanoes erupt?
- Answer the video gives: A volcano erupts when hot rising magma and its dissolved gas build up more pressure than the rock above can hold, so the top of the pipe blows open.
- Hero prop: magma_chamber
- Reviewer: PASS

One cutaway volcano stays big in the middle of the frame. Its glowing chamber slowly fills and heats up, bubbles form in the magma and push it up the pipe, and a pressure gauge swings into the red as the analogy. Then the pipe blows open and lava sprays high, fades, and leaves a calm cone with a new small island. The temperature shows as text on screen while it is said, and the picture ends with the chamber glowing again as the viewer is asked to comment.
